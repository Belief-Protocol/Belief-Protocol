# Corpus

The tradition's own texts. Add the editions you work from here, with locators, so an agent can cite a page rather than paraphrase.

- Holy Scripture, in the translation the reader's church uses.
- The Book of Concord (Lutheran).
- The Heidelberg Catechism, Belgic Confession and Canons of Dort (Reformed).
- The Westminster Standards (Presbyterian).
- The Book of Common Prayer and the Thirty-Nine Articles (Anglican).
- The Second London Baptist Confession (1689) and the Baptist Faith and Message (Baptist).
- John Wesley's Sermons and the Methodist Articles of Religion (Methodist).
