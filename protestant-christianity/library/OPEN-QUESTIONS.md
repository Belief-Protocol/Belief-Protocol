# Open questions: protestant-christianity

What this file is not sure of, and what only a competent authority in the tradition can settle.

## Review order

For each passage take one disposition: recognizable · wrong · missing · too strong · not theirs.

1. **The principal.** Whether one file can stand for Protestant Christianity at all, or should be a base that denominational files extend.
2. **Pentecostal and charismatic Christianity**, now a large share of Protestants worldwide, is under-represented by a source set drawn mostly from the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.
3. **Grips.** Whether `Word and sacrament` should be `clenched` for Lutherans and Anglicans.
4. **The six anomalies.** Confirm each is genuinely divided and fairly stated.
5. **The dissonance entries.** Whether each cites the right acknowledgment, and whether others belong.

## Citations to trace first

Augsburg Confession IV, VII, XXVIII · Thirty-Nine Articles I, VI, XXII, XXV · Westminster Confession I.6, I.9, II.1, XX.2, XXIII · Westminster Shorter Catechism Q. 1 · Barmen Declaration, thesis 1 · SBC Resolution on Racial Reconciliation (1995) · ELCA Declaration to the Jewish Community (1994).
