Draft compiled by AI from the texts it cites. Nothing here has been reviewed or endorsed by any
Church leader or body. Every citation is unverified until checked against the text.

**Guessed `grip`, every one.** Assigned by what the standard works and the First Presidency teach as doctrine: `clenched` for the doctrines of the Restoration; `cradled` for commandments and practices whose application leaders adjust; `open` for programs and policies. *clenched:* the five worldview beliefs; The Restoration; Living prophets and apostles; Priesthood authority; Temples and covenants; Marriage and the family; Three degrees of glory. *cradled:* The Word of Wisdom; Service and the gathering. *open:* Church callings and practice.

**Principal.** The Church speaks through its President, the First Presidency and the Quorum of the Twelve. This file is a compilation of what they and the standard works teach; it is not a Church publication. Other movements tracing to Joseph Smith (for example the Community of Christ) are out of scope.
