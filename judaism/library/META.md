**Guessed `grip`, every one.** Assigned by what the streams hold in common: `clenched` for what all treat as foundational; `cradled` for what all hold but interpret very differently; `open` for the streams themselves. *clenched:* the five worldview beliefs; The covenant with Israel; Life comes first; Shabbat and the festivals; Learning; Teshuvah; Tzedakah and justice. *cradled:* Mitzvot and halakhah; The land of Israel; The Messiah and the world to come. *open:* Many streams.

**Principal.** Judaism has no single voice, and many Jews are secular. This file records what the streams share and names, as anomalies, where they divide. A stream's, community's or person's own belief file governs over it.
