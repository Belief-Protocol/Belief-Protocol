Draft compiled by AI from the texts it cites. Nothing here has been reviewed or blessed by any
bishop, synod or Orthodox authority. Every citation is unverified until checked against the text.

**Guessed `grip`, every one.** Assigned by the Church's own distinctions rather than belief by
belief: `clenched` for what the Ecumenical Councils defined or the Creed confesses; `cradled`
for canonical and pastoral teaching applied with economy; `open` for legitimate local
diversity. *clenched:* the five worldview beliefs; The Church is one, holy, catholic and
apostolic; Conciliarity; The Holy Mysteries; The holy icons; The Mother of God and the
communion of saints; The last things. *cradled:* Marriage and monasticism; Strictness and
economy. *open:* Fasting and the calendar.

**Principal.** "The Orthodox Church" is a communion of autocephalous Churches with no single
spokesman. This file records what those Churches hold in common and names, as anomalies and
dissonance, where they differ.
