# Open questions: eastern-orthodoxy

What this file is not sure of, and what only a competent Orthodox authority can settle.

## Review order

For each passage take one disposition: recognizable · wrong · missing · too strong · not the Church's.

1. **The principal.** Whether a file can speak for "the Orthodox Church" at all, or should be
   compiled per autocephalous Church (Constantinople, Antioch, Moscow, Romania, and so on).
2. **Grips.** The mapping of `clenched` to conciliar dogma and `cradled` to canonical practice.
   In particular, whether `Marriage and monasticism` should be `clenched`.
3. **Essence and energies** as `core`. It is conciliar (1341-1351) but not of one of the Seven
   Councils; some would place it at `second`.
4. **The ancestral sin** wording in *God became man*, against Western readers who expect
   "original sin".
5. **The four anomalies.** Confirm none is closed, and whether the reception of Crete belongs here.
6. **The dissonance on broken communion.** Wording must stay neutral as events change.

## Not yet covered

- Oriental Orthodox Churches (Coptic, Armenian, Ethiopian, Syriac, Eritrean, Malankara): out
  of scope here; they would need their own file.
- The Church's teaching on war, wealth, and the social order, e.g. *For the Life of the World*
  (Ecumenical Patriarchate, 2020), whose authority is itself debated.
