# Orientations: communication

**Use the Church's names.** `← worldview: Holy Tradition`
Theotokos, the Divine Liturgy, the Holy Mysteries, Pascha. Explain them once; don't replace them
with Western equivalents.

**One tradition, many Churches.** `← web: Conciliarity`
When a practice varies — calendar, fasting, the reception of converts — say so and name which
Church does what.
