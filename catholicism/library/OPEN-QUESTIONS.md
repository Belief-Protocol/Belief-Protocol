# Open questions: catholicism

Carried forward from the compiler's notes on the registry version, with review items added for the Belief Protocol examples.

What this file is not sure of, and what only a competent authority in the tradition can settle.

## Review order

For each passage take one disposition: recognizable · wrong · missing · too strong · not theirs.

1. **The principal.** The file names the Catholic Church as principal, but no reviewer can make it the Church's teaching. Consider reframing it as a reading of Catholic teaching for agents, and narrowing `scope` from acting *for* the Church to speaking *about* her teaching.
2. **The name.** Renamed from `roman-catholic-church` to `catholicism` for the examples; the Church's own name for herself is the Catholic Church, and the file's *Two lungs* orientation warns against equating Catholic with Roman.
3. **The Church belief's move from the center to the web.** The original placed the Church among the answers to the five questions on purpose.
4. **Grips.** Thirteen `clenched` against the grades of assent, and `open` on *Catholics of good will legitimately disagree*. Check euthanasia's placement: the grades table lists it as definitively proposed while EV 57 sits under divinely revealed.
5. **Citations.** Every source line says unverified. Start with those the ten orientations rest on.
6. **The five anomalies.** Confirm the Church has closed none of them.
7. **The ten orientations**, restored from the original after an earlier 1,500-character budget cut them to five; at the current budget only their names are always loaded.
8. **Lost companion files.** The original package's `orientations/` (ethics, celebration, prayer, social-political, communication) and `references/` (frameworks, thinkers) were not in the registry export. Recover and restore them if possible.
9. **Quote rather than paraphrase.** The Church's own wording exists for nearly every belief (the Catechism, the councils). Replace close paraphrase with short verbatim quotations where the license allows.
