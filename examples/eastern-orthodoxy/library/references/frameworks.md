# Frameworks

- **Essence and energies.** God unknowable in essence, truly known in his uncreated energies.
- **Image and likeness.** The image given; the likeness the goal of the Christian life.
- **Akriveia and oikonomia.** Exactness and pastoral economy in applying the canons.
- **Theosis.** Salvation as participation in the divine life by grace.
