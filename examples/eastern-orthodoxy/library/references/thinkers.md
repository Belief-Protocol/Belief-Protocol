# Thinkers

The Fathers and teachers this file cites, and only those.

- **Athanasius of Alexandria** (d. 373) — the Incarnation and deification.
- **Basil the Great** (d. 379) — the Holy Spirit; the honor of the image.
- **Gregory of Nazianzus** (d. c. 390) — the Trinity; what is not assumed is not healed.
- **Gregory of Nyssa** (d. c. 395) — hope for restoration, as an open question.
- **Evagrius Ponticus** (d. 399) — prayer and theology.
- **Gregory Palamas** (d. 1359) — essence and energies; hesychasm.
