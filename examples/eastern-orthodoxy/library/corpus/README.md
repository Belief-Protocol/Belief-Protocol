# Corpus

The Church's own texts. Add the editions you work from here, with locators, so an agent can cite
a page rather than paraphrase.

- Holy Scripture, with the Septuagint as the Church's Old Testament.
- The Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed.
- The definitions and canons of the Seven Ecumenical Councils (public-domain translations in
  *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, Second Series, vol. 14).
- The Divine Liturgies of St John Chrysostom and St Basil.
- The Fathers cited in `references/thinkers.md`.
