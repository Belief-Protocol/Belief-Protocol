church, denomination or confessional body. Every citation is unverified until checked against the text.

**Guessed `grip`, every one.** Assigned by what the Reformation confessions hold in common: `clenched` for the creeds and the five solas; `cradled` for what most churches hold but some practice differently; `open` for polity and worship. *clenched:* the five worldview beliefs; The Church is where the Word is preached; The priesthood of all believers; The Great Commission; The last things. *cradled:* Word and sacrament; Conversion and new life; Church and state. *open:* Many churches, one Lord.

**Principal.** "Protestant Christianity" has no single voice. This file records the common confessional core of the Reformation churches and names, as anomalies, where they divide. A denomination's or congregation's own belief file governs over it.
